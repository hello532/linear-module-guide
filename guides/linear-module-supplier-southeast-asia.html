Sourcing linear modules direct from the factory

A short checklist for machine builders who want to go straight to the manufacturer instead of through a distributor.

What to prepare before you write

A factory quote is only as good as the inputs. Prepare these five answers and the first reply can be a proposal instead of a question:

ItemWhat to write
Effective strokeThe usable travel in mm, plus the envelope you have
Load and orientationMoving mass, its offset from the carriage, horizontal or vertical mounting
SpeedThe speed you need at the carriage; lead sets the trade against thrust and resolution
AccuracyThe repeatability your process needs; positioning accuracy if the axis measures
EnvironmentDust, chips, glue splash or corrosion risk — this picks the enclosure

If you are matching a module already designed into a machine, add the drawing or mounting pattern. KINX modules are built with matching dimensions for THK SKR/KR, PMI KM and MISUMI C-KS series. Send your drawing and the fit is checked against the module before you order.

Mounting dimensions are confirmed against your drawing, model by model. Do not assume a fit from a series name alone — send the drawing and let it be checked.

What to ask back

  • The dimensional drawing for the exact model and stroke.
  • The accuracy figures, stated separately for repeatability and positioning accuracy.
  • The stroke range of the series you are being quoted — which lengths are standard and which are custom.
  • The motor adaptation (servo wattages and stepper frame sizes).
  • The delivery time for your quantity, and whether a rush option exists.
  • Which applications the modules have already shipped into.

Why go direct

Three things are different when you buy from the manufacturer rather than a reseller: custom stroke lengths are routine rather than special orders, the accuracy and stroke figures come from the people who build the axis, and the mounting dimensions can be checked against your drawing before the order is placed. The factory page and the contact page on the product site cover the rest.
